Universal Data Extractor Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Universal Data Extractor (the “Extension”), a browser extension published by Avenro Labs, handles information when you use it. The Extension is designed to extract tables, lists, and other structured page data and to export that data locally as CSV, Excel (XLSX), or JSON files.

Universal Data Extractor is built to process website content locally in your browser. Extracted page content is not transmitted to Avenro Labs.

2. Data processed by the extension

The Extension processes content from the web page you choose to scan only when you take an explicit action such as Scan Page or Export. Depending on your selections, this may include:

  • HTML table content and related cell values
  • ARIA table and grid content
  • Repeated structured data detected on the page
  • Field names and values you select or reorder for export
  • Optionally, link URLs and image URLs when you enable those options

The Extension does not require an account. It does not continuously monitor your browsing history.

3. How data is used

Data processed by the Extension is used solely to perform the extraction and export features you request in the moment of use. Generated CSV, JSON, and XLSX files are created locally in your browser for you to download or save.

Processed page content is not used for advertising, profiling, determining creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to providing the Extension’s extraction and export functionality.

4. Data transmission

Extracted website content is processed locally in your browser. Extracted content is not sent to Avenro Labs servers. Extracted content is not sent to third-party servers by the Extension.

5. Data storage

The Extension does not transmit extracted page content to Avenro Labs for storage. Export files (CSV, JSON, and XLSX) are generated locally on your device. Any files you choose to save remain under your control on your device or in locations you select.

6. Third-party sharing

Avenro Labs does not sell user data. The Extension does not share extracted page content with third parties.

7. Analytics and advertising

The Extension does not include analytics services, advertising SDKs, or user tracking mechanisms.

8. User control

You control when extraction runs by initiating actions such as Scan Page or Export. You can choose which fields to include and how they are ordered before export. You may stop using the Extension or uninstall it at any time through your browser’s extension management settings.

9. Permissions

The Extension uses browser permissions only as needed to provide its features:

  • activeTab — temporary access to the active tab so the Extension can read page content when you request a scan or export.
  • scripting — used after a user action to run local extraction code in the page context for the requested scan or export. The Extension does not use remote code execution.

The Extension does not continuously monitor browsing activity across sites.

10. Security

Because extracted content is processed locally and is not transmitted to Avenro Labs or third-party servers by the Extension, exposure related to network transmission of that content is avoided by design. You remain responsible for how you store, share, or further process any files you export.

11. Limited Use

Data accessed or processed through the Extension’s use of browser permissions is used only to provide and improve user-facing features that are prominent in the Extension’s user interface — specifically local extraction and export of structured page data. That data is not sold. It is not used for serving advertisements. It is not used for creditworthiness determinations or unrelated profiling. It is not transferred to third parties for purposes unrelated to providing the Extension’s core functionality.

12. Changes to this policy

Avenro Labs may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the Extension after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
